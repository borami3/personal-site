Stories · Podcast in production

The Market We Built, The Stores We Don't Own

An Exploration of Korean-Owned Black Beauty Supply Stores in Delaware

With the Stanford Braden Storytelling Grant, I'm producing a narrative podcast episode on the relationship between Korean-American shop owners and Black customers in the Black hair care industry, and on what e-commerce is changing. It's built from interviews with store owners, the industry's trade association and scholars.
